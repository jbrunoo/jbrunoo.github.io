GradeAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Overview

GradeAI is provided by JBRUNOO. GradeAI is a math game where users solve addition and subtraction problems and review AI grading results.

Data we process

  • Gameplay progress, settings, scores, coins, achievements, and related app state stored on your device.
  • Advertising-related data may be processed by Google AdMob when ads are shown in the app.
  • Technical information may be processed as needed to provide app updates, ads, app stability, and security.

How we use data

We use data to provide gameplay features, remember local progress, show ads, improve reliability, and respond to privacy or support inquiries.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be processed by service providers only as needed for ads, app operation, security, and reliability.

Retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Data handled by third-party services is retained according to those providers' policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact method listed for GradeAI on Google Play.
