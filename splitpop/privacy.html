Privacy Policy

SplitPop Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Overview

SplitPop is provided by JBRUNOO. This policy explains how SplitPop handles data when you use the app.

Data we process

  • Gameplay progress, preferences, and settings stored on your device.
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data processed by Google AdMob to show and measure ads.
  • App analytics and diagnostics, including events, crash reports, device information, and performance data processed through Google Firebase services when enabled in the release build.

How we use data

We use data to provide game features, remember local progress, show ads, understand app performance, diagnose crashes, prevent abuse, and improve SplitPop.

Third-party services

SplitPop may use Google AdMob for advertising and Google Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics for analytics and crash reporting. These providers may process data under their own privacy policies and terms.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be shared with service providers only as needed for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, security, and app operation.

Retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Data processed by third-party services is retained according to those providers' policies and account settings.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect data associated with the app. Data transmitted to third-party services is handled through those providers' SDKs and infrastructure.

Children

SplitPop is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact method listed for SplitPop on Google Play.
