Checkmate Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Overview

Checkmate is provided by JBRUNOO. Checkmate is designed as a local-first checklist app. The checklists and items you create are stored on your device unless a feature clearly says otherwise.

Data we process

  • Checklist projects, checklist items, completion states, dates, reminders, and app settings you create in the app.
  • Local usage events used inside the app to improve the product experience. These events are stored on your device.
  • Basic network request information when the app connects to the Checkmate template/catalog server, such as request time and technical logs needed to operate the service.

How we use data

We use data to provide checklist features, save your progress, show templates, improve reliability, and respond to support or privacy inquiries.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Checkmate does not require an account for its core local checklist features. Server logs may be processed by infrastructure providers that help operate the service.

Retention and deletion

Data stored locally remains on your device until you edit it, delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Server-side operational logs are kept only as long as reasonably needed for security, debugging, and service operation.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect transmitted service data. Local device data is protected by your device and operating system settings.

Children

Checkmate is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact method listed for Checkmate on Google Play.
